# Visionary Community Conduct

Visionary projects welcome constructive, curious, and respectful collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be clear, kind, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and different levels of experience.
- Share enough evidence for others to verify technical claims.
- Keep project spaces focused on useful work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, credential exposure, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.
